Decoder Norm Flag ?Compares the L2 norm of the CrossCoder's decoder vectors: ‖W_dec_A‖ vs ‖W_dec_B‖. A larger norm means the feature contributes more strongly to that model's residual stream. The classification uses the paper formula (Lindsey et al. 2025): Δnorm = 0.5 × (1 + (‖B‖ − ‖A‖) / max(‖A‖, ‖B‖)), range [0, 1]. A = Δnorm below lower threshold (feature dominant in Model A), B = Δnorm above upper threshold (dominant in Model B), BOTH = in between (shared). The thresholds are not fixed — adjust them by dragging the handles on the Δnorm histogram below.
